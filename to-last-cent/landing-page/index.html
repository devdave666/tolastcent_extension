How your cashback — and your data — actually work

To Last Cent is a CJ Affiliate publisher. Here's exactly what that means for you.

How cashback is funded

When you activate cashback, we open a tracked affiliate link through the CJ Affiliate network before sending you to the merchant. If your purchase qualifies, the merchant pays CJ a referral commission, and we share a portion of that commission back to you. Prices you pay never change — cashback comes out of our commission, not your wallet.

What we track

The extension only activates tracking on partner-store domains you visit and records the click, merchant, and resulting order status needed to credit your account. We do not read page content, log keystrokes, or sell your browsing history to third parties.

Disclosure & independence

To Last Cent is an independent publisher within the CJ Affiliate network and is not operated or endorsed by any individual retailer. Cashback rates are set by each merchant's affiliate program and can change or be excluded for certain categories (gift cards, taxes, shipping) at any time.

Your control

You can dismiss the activation banner on any site, disable the extension at any time from Chrome's toolbar, and request full deletion of your account and click history from your dashboard settings.

Frequently asked questions

Is To Last Cent really free? +

Yes. There's no subscription or fee. We keep a small share of the affiliate commission and pass the rest to you as cashback.

Why do I need to click "Activate Cashback"? +

Activation opens the merchant's official CJ Affiliate tracking link so your order can be correctly attributed to your account before you check out.

How long until cashback becomes available? +

It depends on the merchant's return policy — typically 30 to 90 days after purchase confirmation, at which point your balance moves from Pending to Available.
